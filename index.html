Time me: Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Time me is an Android app that times how long each player takes in a board game. It is developed by Lovro Ludvig. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

In short: the app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it does not have internet access. Nothing you enter is sent to me.

Information the app stores

The only information the app keeps is the player names you type in, so that it can suggest recent players next time. Names are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Game timings are kept only while a game is in progress and are not saved.

Information I collect

None. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send player names or anything else to me or to anyone else. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Sharing

I do not collect your information, so I do not share or sell it.

Android backups

If you have turned on Android's device backup, the system may include the app's stored player names in your backup or when you transfer to a new device. Google handles those backups under your Google account and its privacy policy. You can control this in your device's settings.

Google Play

You download the app through Google Play, which processes data under Google's own privacy policy. Google Play Console may show me aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These do not include your player names.

Keeping and deleting your information

Player names stay on your device until you delete them. To remove them, clear the app's storage in your device's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, I will update this page and its effective date.
